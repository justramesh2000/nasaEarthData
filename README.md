#Description
An app that forecasts air quality by integrating real-time TEMPO (NASA's Tropospheric Emissions: Monitoring of Pollution) data with ground based air quality measurements and weather data, and helps people limit their exposure to unhealthy levels of air pollution by showing them local air quality predictions and sending timely alerts.

#Goals
Improve public health
Gain environmental awareness
Present data in a way that's easy to understand, freely available and reliable

#Data Sources
NASA's earth information
TEMPO
Networks like Pandora, OpenAQ, TolNet

#Satellites in space
Sensors on the ground
Weather data

#Features
Present visualization in easy to understand format
Combine information from various sources
Create local air quality forecasts.
Show source of information
Validate Satellite and ground data
Alerts users
Historical trends
Notify target stakeholders
